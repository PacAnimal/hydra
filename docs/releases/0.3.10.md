### Fixed

- **A large clipboard, such as a screenshot, no longer freezes the cursor or typing.** Clipboards over 512 KiB travel in pieces, and the cursor and keys keep moving while they do.
- While a large clipboard is on its way, the clipboard on the machine receiving it is empty, so a paste there gets nothing rather than what was copied before. It fills once the whole clipboard has arrived. If it never does, the clipboard stays empty and an on-screen message says so.
- Moving the cursor between machines while a large clipboard is on its way is safe: the machine the cursor is on gets its clipboard first, and a paste anywhere gets either the new clipboard or nothing, never the old one. Copying something on the receiving machine meanwhile keeps your copy.
