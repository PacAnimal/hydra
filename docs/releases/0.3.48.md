Everything since 0.3.36. The large change is Wayland: Hydra now runs as a master and as a slave on GNOME, KDE and Hyprland. Hydra updates itself, and your Styx relay needs no update.

### Added

- **Wayland support, master and slave.** It started with Hyprland as a master from @luccast (#14). Hydra now uses the desktop portals and libei, as Deskflow and Input Leap do, so the same path serves every Wayland desktop:
  - A master captures input through the InputCapture portal at the edges that can cross, and takes hotkeys at home through GlobalShortcuts. On Hyprland it reads hotkeys from the keyboards when `/dev/input` is readable and `readKeyboardsForHotkeys` is on (the default).
  - A slave injects through the RemoteDesktop portal. On Hyprland, which has no RemoteDesktop portal, it uses the wlroots virtual pointer and keyboard, and can type any Unicode character.
  - The clipboard goes through the portal, with wl-clipboard as the fallback.
  - README.md and docs/CONFIGURATION.md describe the support and its limits.
- **International and non-Latin keyboard layouts on Linux.** Text types in the active layout, and shortcuts such as Ctrl+C and Ctrl+/ work from a Russian, Greek or other non-Latin layout, as they do locally. Latin layouts (Dvorak, German, French) keep their own keys.
- **`macScrollLines` on a macOS slave** (1 to 10, default 1): the lines one wheel notch scrolls. macOS apps scroll one line for each notch, where Windows and X11 apps scroll three. Set it to 3 to make a Mac scroll as far as the master does.

### Changed

- **Relative mouse mode** sends movement unclamped and never crosses an edge, so a game that recentres the pointer turns at the rate your hand moves.

### Fixed

- **Unplugged and new input devices on Linux** (#15, from @luccast). An unplugged or reconnecting device (a wireless receiver) no longer spins a CPU core, and a keyboard or mouse plugged in after Hydra starts is used. Keys held on a device that goes away are released.
- **A master with `embeddedStyxServer` connects at once.** It no longer waits 15 s after a refused first connect to its own relay.
- Hotkeys match with remapped modifiers (Alt and Super swapped, and similar xkb options) on Wayland.
- A KDE slave reports its screens.
- The clipboard of GTK4 apps on an X11 master syncs.
- No stuck modifiers after a return from a remote.
